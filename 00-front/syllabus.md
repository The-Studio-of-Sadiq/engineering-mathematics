# Syllabus

[← Table of Contents](table-of-contents.md)

**Objective:** Develop the mathematical foundation needed to understand, model, analyze, and simulate electrical and electronic systems.

**Boundary:** Book 1 teaches mathematical methods. Applications such as Fourier-based filtering, circuit simulation, and motor modeling are explained in their relevant core or in Applications & Integrations.

## Part I — Mathematical Foundations

**Chapter 1. Mathematical Language and Algebra** — Real and complex number systems · Algebraic manipulation · Equations and inequalities · Polynomials and rational expressions · Exponential and logarithmic functions · Trigonometric functions and identities · Coordinate systems · Functions, domains, and ranges · Inverse and composite functions · Mathematical notation and dimensional consistency

**Chapter 2. Complex Numbers and Engineering Representation** — Rectangular and polar forms · Complex conjugates · Complex arithmetic · Euler's formula · Exponential representation · Roots and powers of complex numbers · Complex plane and geometric interpretation · Applications to AC circuits and phasors · Complex impedance and transfer functions

**Chapter 3. Trigonometry and Periodic Functions** — Angular measurement · Sinusoidal functions · Phase and phase difference · Period, frequency, and angular frequency · Harmonic functions · Trigonometric expansions · Harmonic relationships · Periodic function representation

## Part II — Calculus

**Chapter 4. Differential Calculus** — Limits and continuity · Derivatives and differentiation rules · Chain rule · Implicit differentiation · Partial derivatives · Higher-order derivatives · Taylor and Maclaurin series · Linearization · Rates of change · Engineering optimization · Sensitivity analysis

**Chapter 5. Integral Calculus** — Indefinite and definite integrals · Integration techniques · Improper integrals · Multiple integrals · Integration by parts · Integration by substitution · Numerical integration · Applications to charge, energy, and accumulated quantities · Mean and RMS values of functions

**Chapter 6. Multivariable Calculus** — Functions of several variables · Partial differentiation · Total differentials · Gradient · Directional derivatives · Jacobian matrices · Multiple integration · Coordinate transformations · Cylindrical and spherical coordinates · Constrained optimization · Lagrange multipliers

**Chapter 7. Vector Calculus** — Scalars and vectors · Vector operations · Vector-valued functions · Vector fields · Gradient, divergence, and curl · Line integrals · Surface integrals · Volume integrals · Green's theorem · Stokes' theorem · Divergence theorem · Applications to electromagnetic field theory

## Part III — Linear Algebra

**Chapter 8. Matrices and Linear Systems** — Matrix operations · Matrix multiplication · Determinants · Matrix inverse · Rank · Systems of linear equations · Gaussian elimination · Gauss–Jordan elimination · LU decomposition · Numerical conditioning · Sparse matrices

**Chapter 9. Vector Spaces and Linear Transformations** — Vector spaces · Subspaces · Linear independence · Basis and dimension · Linear transformations · Null space and column space · Orthogonality · Inner products · Projection · Gram–Schmidt orthogonalization · Least-squares approximation

**Chapter 10. Eigenvalues and Eigenvectors** — Characteristic equations · Eigenvalues and eigenvectors · Diagonalization · Matrix powers · Symmetric matrices · Positive-definite matrices · Singular value decomposition · Modal analysis · Applications to system dynamics and stability

## Part IV — Differential Equations

**Chapter 11. Ordinary Differential Equations** — First-order ODEs · Separable equations · Linear first-order equations · Second-order ODEs · Higher-order ODEs · Homogeneous and nonhomogeneous equations · Initial-value problems · Boundary-value problems · Systems of differential equations · Applications to electrical circuits

**Chapter 12. Linear Differential Equations** — Constant-coefficient equations · Characteristic equations · Natural and forced responses · Complementary and particular solutions · Transient and steady-state behavior · Coupled differential equations · State-variable formulation · Applications to RLC circuits and dynamic systems

**Chapter 13. Partial Differential Equations** — Classification of PDEs · Boundary and initial conditions · Separation of variables · Heat equation · Wave equation · Laplace equation · Fourier-series solutions · Applications to fields, diffusion, and heat transfer

## Part V — Transforms and Mathematical Analysis

**Chapter 14. Laplace Transforms** — Definition and existence · Properties · Standard transforms · Inverse Laplace transforms · Partial fractions · Convolution theorem · Initial- and final-value theorems · Solving differential equations · Applications to circuits and control

**Chapter 15. Fourier Series and Fourier Analysis** — Periodic function decomposition · Trigonometric Fourier series · Complex Fourier series · Even and odd functions · Harmonics · Convergence · Fourier coefficients · Power and energy relationships · Applications to periodic signals

**Chapter 16. Fourier Transforms** — Continuous Fourier transform · Inverse transform · Transform properties · Frequency-domain representation · Convolution and multiplication · Spectral analysis · Energy spectral density · Sampling-related mathematical foundations

**Chapter 17. Z-Transforms** — Sequences and discrete-time functions · Definition and properties · Region of convergence · Inverse Z-transform · Difference equations · System-function representation · Discrete-time system analysis

**Chapter 18. Complex Analysis** — Complex functions · Analyticity · Cauchy–Riemann equations · Contour integration · Cauchy's integral theorem · Taylor and Laurent series · Residues · Residue theorem · Applications to engineering analysis

## Part VI — Probability, Statistics, and Stochastic Mathematics

**Chapter 19. Probability** — Sample spaces and events · Conditional probability · Bayes' theorem · Independence · Random variables · Probability distributions · Probability density and mass functions · Cumulative distributions · Expectation and variance

**Chapter 20. Statistical Analysis** — Descriptive statistics · Mean, median, variance, and standard deviation · Covariance and correlation · Common distributions · Sampling · Estimation · Confidence intervals · Hypothesis testing · Regression · Experimental uncertainty

**Chapter 21. Random Processes** — Random sequences and processes · Stationarity · Autocorrelation and cross-correlation · Power spectral density · Gaussian processes · White noise · Ergodicity · Applications to communications and measurement

## Part VII — Numerical and Computational Mathematics

**Chapter 22. Numerical Methods** — Floating-point arithmetic · Numerical error · Root-finding methods · Bisection and Newton–Raphson methods · Interpolation · Polynomial approximation · Numerical differentiation · Numerical integration · Numerical solution of ODEs · Numerical solution of PDEs · Stability and convergence

**Chapter 23. Optimization** — Objective functions · Constraints · Linear programming · Nonlinear optimization · Gradient-based methods · Newton and quasi-Newton methods · Convex optimization fundamentals · Least-squares optimization · Multi-objective optimization · Applications to engineering design

**Chapter 24. Computational Mathematics** — Algorithmic thinking · Symbolic versus numerical computation · Numerical linear algebra · Iterative solvers · Sparse computation · Monte Carlo methods · Parameter estimation · Sensitivity and uncertainty propagation · Reproducible numerical experiments

**Chapter 25. Mathematical Modeling** — Model formulation · State variables and parameters · Dimensional analysis · Scaling and nondimensionalization · Linearization · Model assumptions · Model validation · Parameter identification · Analytical versus numerical solutions
